Project Vision

A 2v2 half-court basketball experience built as a direct companion to the "Nara's Room - Lizzie McGuire" music video. The game captures the high-energy, Y2K "Disney-Core" aesthetic using procedural rendering and authentic character designs.

Art Direction

The visual identity is defined by a mix of hand-drawn sprites and procedural "Cartoon-Tech" effects. We transitioned the game from a generic court to a vibrant playground aesthetic derived from the music video's set.

Technical Highlights

  • Wavy Seam Logic: Procedural sine-wave math creates the signature cartoon look for the basketball's seams.
  • State-Driven CPU: Modular behavior handlers that simulate aggressive driving and strategic shooting.
  • Timing Shot System: A shrinking-ring mechanic that maps player skill directly to point success.
  • Responsive Canvas: Dynamic scaling ensures the Y2K vibe fits everything from a MacBook to an iPhone.
