V3 Art Direction

Half Court Hero

A playable music-video frame: live-action schoolyard footage, rough cartoon basketball overlays, and band-member styling pulled from the “Lizzie McGuire” video world.

North star: video still first, game second
01

Visual Pillars

Live-Action Base

Schoolyard court, chain-link fence, beige brick, blue doors, hard sun, blown highlights, and handheld-video framing. The background should feel captured, not designed.

Cartoon Intervention

Nara and the basketball can be loose 2D overlays. Keep the line work wobbly, flat, and a little awkward, like a cartoon drawn over a paused video.

Band Identity

Use the green racing jersey, WMPG shirt with orange sleeves, striped shirt, beanies, instruments, and casual band poses as the cast language.

Gameplay Feel

Keep

The timing-based shot ring, pass/shoot/block/steal controls, first-to-11 half-court structure, and simple static-page deployment.

Improve

Make shot timing feel like syncing to a beat. Make rebound and turnover outcomes legible. Let defense affect shot quality instead of only binary block/steal checks.

Add Personality

Use freeze frames, rough captions, band quips, basketball taunts, and small pose changes instead of arcade particles or neon celebration effects.

06

Asset Prompt

Create a still frame for a browser basketball game inspired by an early-2000s indie music video. The scene is an outdoor schoolyard basketball court with chain-link fence, beige brick building, blue doors, harsh midday sun, blown highlights, and handheld video texture. Add one rough 2D cartoon woman composited into the scene: black bob haircut, black t-shirt, bright blue athletic shorts, white socks, chunky dark shoes, half-lidded eyes, pursed confident mouth, casually spinning an orange basketball. Style should look like a loose adult-cartoon overlay on a live-action video still. Avoid pixel art, neon arcade UI, fantasy backgrounds, character select screens, polished anime, or glossy vector art.
